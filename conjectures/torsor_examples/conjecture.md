Let X be a smooth and projective complex surface.
Let E be a smooth and projective complex threefold, with a smooth morphism $\pi:E\rightarrow X$.
Suppose that $(E,\pi)$ has the structure of an abelian scheme over $X$.

Then for every integer $p>1$, there exists a tuple $(X,E,\pi)$ and an $E$-torsor $C$ over $X$ 
such that $C$ has period $p$ and index $p^2$.
