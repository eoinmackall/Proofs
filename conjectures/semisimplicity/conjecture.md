**Conjecture (Frobenius semisimplicity).** Let \(q=p^a\) be a prime power, let \(X\) be a smooth projective variety over \(\mathbb F_q\), and let \(\ell\neq p\) be a prime. For every integer \(i\geq0\), geometric Frobenius acts semisimply on
\[
H^i_{\mathrm{\acute et}}
\!\left(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell\right);
\]
equivalently, its action on
\[
H^i_{\mathrm{\acute et}}
\!\left(X_{\overline{\mathbb F}_q},\mathbb Q_\ell\right)
\otimes_{\mathbb Q_\ell}\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell
\]
is diagonalizable. Here geometric Frobenius is the inverse of the automorphism \(x\mapsto x^q\) of \(\overline{\mathbb F}_q\).
